Where the desert
meets the table

ZAYAN means adornment. It is the name for something beautiful made even better by care. Our kitchen takes the dishes of the Levant, the Gulf and North Africa and cooks them with modern technique and a light hand.

Bread still comes from the tannour. Lamb is still slow-cooked over coals. Spices are ground each morning from the same blends Arabian traders carried across the Silk Road. What changes is the plating, the precision and the pace of the evening.

The room is quiet, warm and softly lit, built for long dinners and for people who like to take their time.

Chef Omar Al-Rashid

Executive Chef and Founder

Omar grew up in his grandmother's kitchen in Amman, learning to roast spices before he learned to read recipes. Two decades in Beirut, Paris and London taught him restraint. He came home to Dubai to cook the food of his childhood the way he now sees it.

"Heritage is not something we preserve in a jar. We cook it fresh every night."

The Experience

Three ways to spend an evening at ZAYAN.

The Interior

Carved brass screens, hand-laid zellige tile, and low amber light. A dining room for 80 guests, with a view of the open fire kitchen.

Private Dining

The Majlis seats up to 16 behind carved doors, with its own host, a bespoke tasting menu and a pairing chosen for your table.

Chef's Table

Eight seats at the pass. Watch Chef Omar cook an eight-course journey through Arabia, and ask him anything between courses.
